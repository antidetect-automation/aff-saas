# Multilogin vs Octo Browser: social SOPs vs automation spine

Octo shows up constantly in media-buyer Discord threads. Multilogin shows up when the same threads start talking about scripts, broken sessions, or Android. Both can be serious. They optimize for different Thursdays.

## Octo’s advantage

Social, not mystical: teams already share SOPs, Loom walkthroughs, and hiring filters around it. Swapping the browser without swapping the process is how agencies produce downtime and Slack rage.

## Multilogin’s advantage

The automation spine and the broader product family. If your next quarter includes Playwright jobs, stricter funnels, or Cloud Phone, starting that work on Multilogin X beats bolting adapters onto a stack you outgrew. Free five profiles and cheaper Pro yearly also mean you can parallel-test without a board meeting.

## Practical split

**Keep Octo** when campaigns are human-driven, the crew is Octo-fluent, and you’re not about to industrialize profile lifecycle in code.

**Prefer Multilogin** when profile lifecycle is engineering — tokens, start/stop, CDP attach, proxy lanes logged next to profile UUIDs. That’s the world our [guides](https://antidetect-automation.github.io/guides/) assume.

Hybrid is common: Octo for creatives testing angles, Multilogin for money accounts and bots. Don’t mix cookie jars.
