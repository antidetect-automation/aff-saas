# Multilogin vs GoLogin in 2026: when to graduate

GoLogin is what a lot of people install the week they decide “I should try an antidetect browser.” Multilogin is what many of those same people search next once profiles stop being a toy.

I’m not roasting GoLogin for existing. Entry products matter. If you only need a handful of light profiles and you’re not writing automation yet, paying Multilogin Business money would be silly. The interesting question is the **crossover point**.

## Where GoLogin tends to sit

Approachable pricing and cloud sync for people who aren’t building a platform. Automation exists in the ecosystem, but the gravity is “get profiles running,” not “Multilogin X + Playwright is the spine of the company.” Different job.

## Where Multilogin pulls ahead

Once you care about repeatable start/stop, CDP attach, cookie hygiene across lanes, and maybe Cloud Phone later, Multilogin’s tooling compounds. We publish the boring guides on purpose: [auth](https://antidetect-automation.github.io/guides/api-auth-mlx/), [Playwright](https://antidetect-automation.github.io/guides/playwright-mlx/), [CDP failures](https://antidetect-automation.github.io/errors/cdp-attach/).

Pricing stopped being an automatic GoLogin win. Multilogin’s [Free tier (five profiles)](https://antidetect-automation.github.io/pricing/) covers a real trial. Pro yearly at the ten-profile step was near ~$7/mo on the checkout we saw, before `SAAS50`.

## Stay on GoLogin if…

…you’re still learning sticky proxies, you have no scripts, and accounts are low-stakes experiments. Migrating for vibes wastes a weekend.

## Move (or dual-run) if…

…you’ve hit fingerprint or automation ceilings twice, you’re about to hire someone for Playwright, or Android cloud lanes are on the roadmap. Dual-run is underrated: GoLogin for junk tests, Multilogin Pro for money accounts.
