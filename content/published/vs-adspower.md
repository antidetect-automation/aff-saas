## Day-to-day feel

**AdsPower** grew as a volume workstation. Friendly seat math, in-product automation for “click this flow” without opening VS Code. That muscle memory is real — ripping a VA playbook overnight is usually a bad idea.

**Multilogin’s** center of gravity is Multilogin X: start/stop profiles, attach Playwright or Puppeteer over CDP, keep cookies and proxies aligned. When something fails, it’s often auth, debugger URL timing, or proxy geo — boring ops, not mysticism.

I’m not coronating a permanent public fingerprint lab winner every quarter. What I will say: teams that already trusted Multilogin on stricter targets rarely switch *away* for quality. They switch for price or RPA habit.

## Cloud Phone and “one vendor”

If half your lanes are Android apps, Multilogin’s Cloud Phone SKU matters. AdsPower conversations stay mostly browser-profile. You can duct-tape a second cloud-phone vendor next to AdsPower — people do — then you debug two bills. Multilogin isn’t magic; it’s fewer moving parts when **`MIN50`** is on the table.
