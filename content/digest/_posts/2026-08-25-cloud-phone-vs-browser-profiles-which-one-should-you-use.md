# Cloud Phone vs. Browser Profiles: Which One Should You Use?

At Multilogin, we understand that choosing between cloud phone and browser profiles can be a daunting task, especially when it comes to social media marketing. Our team of experts is here to guide you through the process and help you make an informed decision.

- When using cloud phone, you'll need to ensure that your main workflows happen on mobile-first platforms and require native Android apps. This is ideal for businesses with a strong presence in these spaces.
- Browser profiles, on the other hand, are perfect for social media marketing, as they allow you to access websites and browser extensions without the need for native apps. This is ideal for businesses with a strong focus on these areas.
- One key difference between cloud phone and browser profiles is the level of control you have over your device. Browser profiles give you more control over your browser, while cloud phone requires you to use a native app.
