# eBay Two-Step Verification Not Working? Common Fixes

We've seen many eBay sellers struggle with two-step verification issues. It's essential to understand what's causing the problem and how to resolve it.

- Using a proxy can sometimes lead to two-step verification failures, as eBay may flag the proxy as suspicious.
- Warm-up processes with cookies can also cause issues, especially if the seller is using a new proxy or IP address.
- Browser vs Cloud Phone or API/Pro: eBay's two-step verification may work differently depending on the device or method used, which can lead to inconsistencies.
