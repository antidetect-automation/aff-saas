# The social media approval process checklist that catches what reviewers miss

A post goes out, but its success is often short-lived. Twenty minutes later, someone spots it: a link that 404s, a stat nobody fact-checked, a call-to-action button that's cut off on mobile, or a sponsored post with no disclosure. The approval process technically happened, but the real work begins when the post fails to deliver.

- Proxies can lead to low engagement, as they often mask the origin of the post.
- Warmup/cookies are crucial for building trust with the audience, as they help establish the post's credibility.
- Browser vs Cloud Phone or API/Pro: the choice between these options can significantly impact the post's performance, with browser and Pro generally offering better results.
