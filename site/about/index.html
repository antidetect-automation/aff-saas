About

Who runs this

antidetect-automation is a small independent hub for people who run Multilogin-style multi-account setups. We write English guides and comparisons, then route buying decisions through a Telegram bot that issues SAAS50 (browser) or MIN50 (cloud phone).

We are Multilogin affiliates. If you buy with our codes or links, we may earn a commission. That does not make us Multilogin’s support team, and it does not entitle us to invent “undetectable” claims.

What we optimize for

Operators who automate — Playwright, Puppeteer, API auth, proxies that match locale — and who want fewer scammy coupon blogs. Free Multilogin seats and cheaper Pro pricing in 2026 are documented on /pricing/ because the old “too expensive to try” meme was steering people badly.

What we refuse

Mass DMs, fake engagement, doorway pages that only swap a competitor name, and opening endless GitHub repos that look like spam. New work stays in the existing aff-saas monorepo; the live site is only antidetect-automation.github.io.
