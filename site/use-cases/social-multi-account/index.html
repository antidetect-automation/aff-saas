Social multi-account isolation

One profile ≈ one social identity. This is hygiene for agencies running multiple client brands — not a playbook for spam, fake engagement, or ToS roulette.

Checklist

  1. Sticky proxy matching the persona’s claimed geo — proxy.
  2. Align timezone/language — TZ tool.
  3. Warm before posting bursts — warmup.
  4. Keep cookies in that profile only — cookies.
  5. Automate checklists carefully with Playwright on Pro+; leave sensitive posts to humans.
