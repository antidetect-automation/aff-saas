Does Multilogin have a free plan?

Yes. Five profiles, forever free, browser or cloud mobile. No API, no team seats. If you ghost the product for seven days without starting a profile, those free profiles can disappear. Details and caveats: pricing notes.

What’s Pro cost right now?

On yearly billing, the ten-profile step showed about $85 / year (~$7.08 / month) when we last checked. Fifty and hundred profile steps cost more. Monthly billing is higher than yearly. Multilogin can change this without emailing us first.

SAAS50 and MIN50?

SAAS50 = browser coupon path. MIN50 = Cloud Phone. Grab them from the Telegram bot. We’re affiliates; we may get paid if you use the codes. Your checkout line item is what counts.

Playwright on Free?

No. Free has no API. Pro or above. Pattern: Playwright + Multilogin X.
