Cookies & sessions

Most “fingerprint failed” panics on ads desks are cookie stories: you pasted yesterday’s BM jar into today’s profile, or three buyers shared one Chromium storage and then acted surprised.

Rules that keep money lanes alive

  1. One Multilogin profile ≈ one login you care about. Not “the agency Chrome.”
  2. Never merge cookies across unrelated Business Managers or MCC lanes.
  3. Export/import only when you understand what you overwrite — Multilogin’s Postman collection has Browser Profile Data / cookie folders; paths move — official API.
  4. After proxy changes, expect some sessions to invalidate. That is normal.

Warmth vs stolen jars

Warm cookies you earned inside a coherent geo/proxy story. Buying mystery cookie packs and dumping them into Multilogin is how you discover platform abuse systems the expensive way. We don’t facilitate that.

Playwright / API ops

When you attach over CDP, you inherit that profile’s jar — do not context.clearCookies() for “clean tests” on money lanes. Sacrificial QA profiles exist for a reason. Token problems are separate: token desk.

Quick failures

  • Imported cookies from another geo → sudden logouts and 2FA storms.
  • Shared team profile for “just checking Ads Manager” → BM soup.
  • Cloud Phone vs browser jars are not interchangeable.
