Cookies & sessions

Treat each Multilogin profile as one identity store. Mixing warmed cookies across unrelated accounts defeats isolation.

Rules of thumb

  • One profile → one long-lived session narrative
  • Export/import cookies only with matching proxy geo + fingerprint era
  • After proxy changes, expect re-auth; don’t force old cookies blindly
  • Automate with Playwright only after manual warm looks normal
