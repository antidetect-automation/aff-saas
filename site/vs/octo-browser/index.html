Multilogin vs Octo Browser

Octo shows up constantly in media-buyer Discord threads. Multilogin shows up when the same threads start talking about scripts, broken sessions, or Android. Both can be “serious.” They optimize for different Thursdays.

Octo’s advantage is social, not mystical: teams already share SOPs, Loom walkthroughs, and hiring filters around it. Swapping the browser without swapping the process is how agencies produce downtime and Slack rage.

Multilogin’s advantage is the automation spine and the broader product family. If your next quarter includes Playwright jobs, stricter funnels, or Cloud Phone, starting that work on Multilogin X beats bolting adapters onto a stack you outgrew. Free five profiles and cheaper Pro yearly also mean you can parallel-test without a board meeting.

Practical split

Keep Octo when campaigns are human-driven, the crew is Octo-fluent, and you’re not about to industrialize profile lifecycle in code.

Prefer Multilogin when profile lifecycle is an engineering problem — tokens, start/stop, CDP attach, proxy lanes logged next to profile UUIDs. That’s the world our guides assume.

Hybrid happens all the time: Octo for creatives testing angles, Multilogin for the money accounts and the bots. Just don’t mix cookie jars.
