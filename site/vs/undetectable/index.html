Multilogin vs Undetectable

The product name does marketing heavy lifting. Nothing in this category is undetectable. What you’re really choosing is how you spend Tuesday: turning spoof knobs, or shipping jobs against an API you can trust enough to automate.

Undetectable tends to attract people who enjoy configuration depth — packs, toggles, “one more fingerprint tweak.” That’s a valid hobby and sometimes a valid research workflow. It becomes expensive when your business needs repeatable start/stop and CDP attach more than another slider.

Multilogin’s bet is production rhythm. Authenticate, start profile, connect Playwright, stop profile, log the UUID. We document the dull parts because that’s where nights get lost: auth, CDP, proxies. Cloud Phone sits in the same family if Android shows up later.

Try-before-you-sermon is easier now. Multilogin’s Free plan covers five profiles; Pro yearly isn’t the old luxury-object meme. Stack SAAS50 when you pay for browser seats — and still judge outcomes by accounts kept alive, not by how elaborate the spoof panel looks.

I’d stay on Undetectable if…

…you’re actively researching fingerprint surfaces, you aren’t building a job queue yet, and the team likes living in that UI. Migrating mid-experiment for affiliate discourse is silly.

I’d lean Multilogin if…

…automation is the product, several people need a shared vendor story, or you’re tired of “one more config” delaying shipping. Then use Free to compare feel, Pro when you need API, codes from the desk.

Name vs outcomes

Judge by kept accounts and shipped jobs, not by how many spoof toggles you can turn. Our ads and API essays assume that production mindset: ads · Playwright.

Name vs ops reality

Marketing that sounds like insulation is not a plan. Isolation, sticky proxies, and paced warmup beat brand adjectives — warmup · proxy. Multilogin Free still lets you A/B without a leap of faith.
