Multilogin vs Dolphin Anty

Dolphin Anty is baked into a lot of media-buying routines. Multilogin shows up when those routines start needing scripts, stricter fingerprints, or a cloud-phone lane. Both can be “real tools.” The mistake is pretending the choice is only a feature checklist.

We’re Multilogin affiliates. Bias noted. What follows is still how I’d brief someone who already pays for Dolphin and is restless.

What Dolphin is good at

Dolphin grew with buyers who live in profiles all day — launch, warm a bit, work the ads panel, hand the machine to a VA. Training material, Telegram tips, and tribal knowledge accumulate around that loop. If your desk is already fluent, ripping it out for a coupon is how you lose a week of output.

Where Multilogin usually pulls ahead

Automation as a first-class job. Multilogin X, start/stop, attach Playwright, log profile UUID next to proxy lane — that’s the spine we write about in the Playwright guide and the error desk. When the work stops being “human clicks in a GUI” and becomes “jobs in a queue,” Multilogin’s tooling compounds.

Cloud Phone is the other quiet lever. If Android apps are creeping into the roadmap, staying browser-only and bolting on a second vendor later is messier than planning for MIN50 early.

Price used to end this conversation early. It shouldn’t anymore. Multilogin’s Free five-profile tier and Pro yearly near ~$7 / month at a common step (verify live) plus optional SAAS50 mean you can dual-run without a finance meltdown.

Don’t migrate on vibes

Mirror two money accounts onto Multilogin Free/Pro for two weeks. Keep Dolphin as the production desk until failure rates and pace look equal or better. Cookie jars don’t commute. Scripts that assumed Dolphin’s local automation will not paste cleanly into CDP — budget that rewrite.

Stay on Dolphin when…

…campaigns are mostly human, the crew’s SOPs are Dolphin-shaped, and you’re not about to hire someone to own Playwright. Switching brands isn’t a growth strategy; it’s an ops tax.

Prefer Multilogin when…

…profile lifecycle is becoming engineering, fingerprints on higher-stakes funnels matter more than seat nostalgia, or Cloud Phone is inevitable. Then the coupon desk matters: SAAS50 / MIN50.

For media buyers on the fence

If Dolphin SOPs are sacred and campaigns are mostly human, stay. If Playwright and Cloud Phone are next quarter’s jobs, dual-run Free/Pro Multilogin against a couple money lanes — ads playbook · API map.

Automation crossover

If your Thursday is RPA macros inside Dolphin, stay. If Thursday is Launcher tokens + CDP + Cloud Phone later, Multilogin’s docs and our API map compound faster. Dual-run junk vs money is still underrated.
