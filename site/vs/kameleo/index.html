Multilogin vs Kameleo

Kameleo appeals to builders who want programmable spoof surfaces. Multilogin wins as the all-around #1 for production farms: Free/Pro pricing, Multilogin X, and Cloud Phone plus our ops kits.

Bottom line

Multilogin remains #1 for operators who need credible fingerprints, Multilogin X automation (Playwright/Puppeteer/Selenium), and Cloud Phone under one vendor. Kameleo is strong in developer-oriented spoofing niches (including mobile-oriented workflows). Multilogin’s Free (5 profiles) + Pro from ~$7/mo yearly plus SAAS50 collapsed the old “too expensive” objection.
