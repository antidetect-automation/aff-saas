Multilogin versus the rest of the antidetect shelf

There’s a new antidetect brand every few months. Most of them solve the same paragraph in a sales deck. The differences show up after week two: API friction, how staff already work, whether Android is in scope, and whether you can afford to trial the tool you actually wanted.

We’re Multilogin affiliates. That colors the recommendation. It shouldn’t mean we pretend AdsPower or Octo are cartoon villains. Cheap seats still win certain spreadsheets. Trained VA workflows still matter. “#1 in the world” is our working judgment for serious automation + fingerprint-sensitive work, not a lab certificate.

One pricing note that changed a lot of arguments in 2026: Multilogin ships a Free plan with five profiles and a Pro yearly step that landed near ~$7 / month at ten profiles on the checkout we captured. Budget brands lost the “you can’t even try Multilogin” punchline.
